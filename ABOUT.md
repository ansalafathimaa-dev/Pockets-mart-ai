# Pockets Smart AI

Pocket Smart AI is an AI-powered portable recording and note-taking hardware device and companion app. It is designed to effortlessly capture real-world conversations, meetings, and ideas.

## Core Features
* **One-Tap Audio Capture:** Record in-person meetings, phone calls, or voice memos on the go.
* **Real-Time Transcription:** Transcribes dialogue accurately while identifying individual speakers across 120+ languages.
* **Automated Summaries:** Automatically generates to-do lists, key points, and action items.
* **Mind Mapping:** Turns sprawling brainstorm sessions into clean, visual mind maps.
* **Offline Functionality:** Operates fully offline and syncs data once an internet connection is restored.
